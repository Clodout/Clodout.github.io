Help with Clodout, imports, exports, and app behavior.

Contact

For support, bug reports, or privacy questions, email haseeb.kn@outlook.com.

Helpful details to include

Your Clodout version, platform and operating system version, device model, what you expected to happen, and the steps that led to the problem. Do not send wardrobe photos or backup files unless they are necessary and you are comfortable sharing them.

Source and issue tracking

Clodout's public source repositories and issue trackers are available through the Clodout organization on GitHub.

Your data

Clodout stores wardrobe data locally and does not provide remote account recovery. Keep a recent full backup before moving devices, removing the app, or clearing its data.
